## Biz-Teroids
Biz-Teroids allows you to resupply and restock all your businesses with a single click as default, but you can adjust the following options through the **Control Panel**:

- Instant Max Restock.
- Hangar Set Good.
  - Hangar Good type.
- Warehouse Set Good.
  - Warehouse Good type.
- MC Businesses Resupply.
- MC Businesses Restock.
- Nightclub Restock.

<sub>"**x** Set Good" lets you to select the specific type of good that your Warehouses and Hangar will be restocked with.</sub>

The Control Panel is not just a group of few toggles, it also lets you source **Special Items** for your warehouses, trigger **Alien Egg** resupply mission for your Bunker, teleport to your businesses and more!

If you prefer to restock your businesses individually, you can do so under the **Individual Features** section, which also gives you access the **Master Control Terminal** so you manage your businesses remotely.

*Biz-Teroids* also lets you:<br>
**Instant-Buy**: Finish buy / steal missions instantly and set the amount of goods to obtain.
**Instant-Sell**: Finish sell missions instantly. *Wait a few seconds before pressing the button, don't just spam it.

## Human Canvas
This category allows you to **Stack Tattoos**, Makeup/Facepaints and Blusher, and apply **hidden colors** to your character's Hair.

It also supports saving and loading tattoos.
Tattoos get saved to **Exported_TattooCombo.txt** in a regular format so they can be imported through the built-in **Stat Editor**.

If you end up with a nice combo, feel free to [share it](https://www.unknowncheats.me/forum/grand-theft-auto-v/727723-stack-tattoos-complete-list-stats-values.html)!
